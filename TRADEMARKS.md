# Anastom Trademark Policy

## Purpose

The Anastom name and official logos identify software, releases, and community activities produced or approved by the Anastom project. This policy protects users from confusion while allowing the community to discuss, teach, extend, support, and interoperate with Anastom.

This policy covers trademark use only. Copyright permissions come from the software and documentation licenses. It does not represent that a mark is registered in any jurisdiction.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- truthfully state that software uses, supports, integrates with, or is compatible with Anastom;
- link to the official repository and reproduce an unmodified project logo only to identify or link to the official project;
- use the name in reviews, comparisons, research, books, articles, tutorials, issue reports, and discussions;
- describe services as consulting, support, training, or hosting **for Anastom** when the presentation clearly identifies the provider as independent;
- run a local community group or noncommercial event using a name such as “Anastom users in Seattle,” with a clear independent-community disclaimer; and
- redistribute an unmodified official release as “Anastom,” while preserving its notices and making no claim of project endorsement.

Use only as much of a mark as needed to identify the project. Do not imitate the project's overall presentation or place the official logo more prominently than your own identity.

## Uses that require written permission

Written permission is required to:

- name a product, service, company, package, domain, application, conference, or certification with “Anastom” or a confusingly similar term;
- brand a modified distribution or fork as Anastom;
- use an official logo as part of another product or organization's logo;
- describe an offering as official, approved, certified, preferred, or endorsed by the project; or
- manufacture merchandise bearing an Anastom mark for sale.

Modified distributions and forks are welcome under the project license, but they must use a distinct primary name. They may say “based on Anastom” or “compatible with Anastom” if the statement is accurate and no endorsement is implied.

## Official and compatibility claims

Only artifacts published through project-controlled repositories, package namespaces, and release processes are official Anastom releases. A public compatibility or certification program, if created, will use documented, vendor-neutral conformance criteria. Sponsorship or payment alone will never establish compatibility or official status.

## Requests and reporting confusion

Until a separate legal steward and contact are named in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), direct permission requests and reports of confusing use to the repository owner through the contact information on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/gsornsen).

Permission for one use does not imply permission for another. The project may revise this policy for future uses, but a written permission already granted remains governed by its terms.
